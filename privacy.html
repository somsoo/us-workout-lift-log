Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how Simple Workout & Lift Tracker operates regarding user data.

1. 100% Local Client-Side Operation

All input values, calculations, files, and simulations performed on this tool remain strictly inside your web browser memory. No private financial, salary, personal, or tax data is transmitted to or stored on our servers.

2. Cookies and Advertising

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements. Google and its third-party advertising partners may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this website or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings.

3. Your Workout Logs and Exercise Records Run Locally

All set weights, repetition counts, estimated 1RM calculations, and historical training sessions are processed and stored exclusively in your local browser storage. No workout data or exercise histories are uploaded to remote servers.
